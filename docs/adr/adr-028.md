# ADR-028: Client-side AI quota guard

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Related:** REQ-014 · T17

## Context

Free tiers have small, changeable per-minute and per-day limits. Exhausting quota mid-job must not burn retry attempts or fail jobs.

## Decision

- Per provider+model: a token-bucket RPM limiter plus a daily request counter computed from `provider_usage` against `rpd_limit`, reset at the provider's reset time (midnight Pacific for Gemini, UTC day for OpenRouter).
- Before a stage starts it reserves the calls it needs; if short, the job is rescheduled to after the reset (`QuotaDeferred` with explicit `next_attempt_at`), without consuming an attempt.
- 429 responses honor `Retry-After`.

## Consequences

- Jobs wait for quota instead of failing.
- `rpm_limit` / `rpd_limit` must be filled in from AI Studio before the pilot.
