# ADR-033: Subject-aware reframing with a blurred-background fallback

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Related:** REQ-010 · supersedes the crop part of ADR-013

## Context

ADR-013 center-crops every clip to 720×1280. From a 16:9 source that keeps only the middle 32% of the width, so a speaker framed off-center is cut in half, and in a two-person shot both people can disappear. The spec lists subject-aware reframing as a Could-have. The VPS has 2 vCPUs, 4 GB RAM and no GPU.

## Decision

- A job setting, `subject_reframing_enabled` (default `false`), turns reframing on. Off keeps ADR-013's center crop, so both can be compared before switching the default.
- Before rendering each clip, one ffmpeg pass over the clip range writes 640-px-wide frames at 2 fps and reports scene cuts (`scene > 0.3`). Cuts less than 1 s from the previous cut or from the clip's end are ignored.
- Faces are detected on CPU with OpenCV's YuNet model (`opencv-python-headless`, model file vendored under `clipforge/media/models/`, MIT license), with a minimum score of 0.8.
- Each shot gets one fixed framing:
  - If every face seen anywhere in the shot fits inside a 9:16 window, the clip is cropped at a position that holds them all, centred on them and clamped to the frame.
  - Otherwise, or when no face is found, the whole frame is scaled to 720 px wide over a blurred, zoomed copy of itself.
- The render uses one filtergraph. The crop's x position follows the shots, and the blurred-fit branch is overlaid only during the shots that need it.
- The sampled frames are deleted once planning ends. The plan isn't stored: a re-render recomputes it, and the result is the same.
- Sources no wider than 9:16 keep the center crop.

## Consequences

- People the detector finds are never cut. When they can't share one crop, the shot shrinks instead of losing someone.
- A person whose face isn't detected (seen from behind, in profile, very small) is not protected. Shots without detected faces fall back to the blurred layout, so this only matters when someone else's face is detected.
- Fallback shots show people at about a third of the screen height.
- Each clip costs one extra low-resolution decode plus face detection, and clips with blurred shots also cost the blur branch. Benchmark render time on the VPS before making this the default.
- The backend install grows by about 45 MB (OpenCV and numpy wheels).
