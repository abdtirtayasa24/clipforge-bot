# ADR-027: Free-tier AI providers: Gemini primary, OpenRouter fallback

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Related:** REQ-007, REQ-008, REQ-018 · T18, T21, T32

## Context

Product owner requirement (2026-10-19): use free APIs (Gemini API or OpenRouter). Checked against official docs on 2026-10-19 (Gemini pricing and model pages, OpenRouter limits and model list). Google does not publish fixed free-tier quotas; they are shown per project in AI Studio and were reportedly cut several times in 2026.

## Decision

| Capability | Primary (free) | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | Gemini `gemini-3.5-transcribe` (file mode, word timestamps, Indonesian `id-ID` supported, ≤ 30 min/request with timestamps) | `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` with audio input (segment-level timing only) |
| Viral analysis per window | Gemini `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (stable, multimodal input, JSON-schema output, 1M context) | OpenRouter `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free` (text + image, OpenAI-compatible API) |
| Final re-rank with frames (1 call/video) | Gemini `gemini-3-flash-preview` | `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` when its quota is used up |
| Affiliate embeddings | Gemini `gemini-embedding-2` | Keyword fallback (ADR-020) |

- All run behind the protocols in ADR-009, so moving to paid tiers or other providers is a settings change.
- Model IDs are configuration, not code.

## Consequences

- Expected volume: a 120-minute video needs about 6 transcription + 12–14 window-analysis + 1 re-rank calls. 5 videos/day (~100 calls) is plausible on free tiers; 20/day probably is not. Real capacity is measured in T42.
- OpenRouter free models: 20 requests/min; 50 requests/day with fewer than 10 credits ever purchased, 1,000/day after. They do not transcribe audio.
- **Data use:** on the Gemini free tier (and many OpenRouter free endpoints) prompts and responses are used to improve the provider's products. Creators' audio, transcripts and frames would be shared; creator authorization must allow this, otherwise switch to the paid tier.
- Preview/free models may be renamed or removed; readiness checks flag missing models.
