# ADR-024: AI cost tracking via price snapshots

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Related:** Should-Have (AI cost tracking) · T17, T30, T35

## Context

Providers bill differently (tokens vs audio minutes), and prices change. Reports must stay reproducible.

## Decision

- Each `ai_provider_configs` row carries admin-editable pricing (`price_input_per_mtok`, `price_output_per_mtok`, `price_per_audio_minute`, `currency`); free-tier models are priced at 0.
- Every provider call writes a `provider_usage` row (job, stage, provider, model, latency, tokens or audio seconds, `estimated_cost`) using the price **snapshotted into the job**.
- Costs are labeled "estimated".

## Consequences

- Later price edits never rewrite history.
- Estimates can drift from invoices; compared during the pilot.
