# ADR-031: Source registration without permission evidence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Related:** REQ-005, REQ-006 · supersedes the evidence part of ADR-017

## Context

ADR-017 had every upload and permitted-URL registration declare permission evidence: a basis, a statement of at least 10 characters and an optional reference. Product owner decision (2026-10-21): the form step is friction for a single administrator who only processes media they may use, so the requirement is removed.

## Decision

- `POST /uploads` and `POST /videos/{id}/media` no longer accept a `permission` object.
- `media_assets.permission_evidence` keeps only who registered the source and when (`recorded_by`, `recorded_at`). The column is not changed.
- The other source controls stay: the permitted-domain allowlist and SSRF guard (ADR-018), the refusal of YouTube hosts, and validation with ffprobe.

## Consequences

- Uploading or registering a URL takes one step fewer.
- The database no longer records why a source may be processed. The administrator alone is responsible for having that right.
- Sources registered before this change keep their recorded evidence.
