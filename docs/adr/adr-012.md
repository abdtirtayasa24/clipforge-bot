# ADR-012: Audio extraction and chunking

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Related:** REQ-007 · T16, T18

## Context

`gemini-3.5-transcribe` accepts up to 30 minutes of audio per request when word-level timestamps are enabled, and free-tier daily quotas make request count matter.

## Decision

- Extract mono 16 kHz compressed audio.
- Split into chunks of configurable length (default **20 min**, hard max 28 min) with ~2 s overlap.
- Normalize per-chunk timestamps to absolute time and de-duplicate words in overlaps.

## Consequences

- A 120-minute video needs about 6 transcription requests.
- Timestamp merging must be well tested because cut accuracy depends on it.
